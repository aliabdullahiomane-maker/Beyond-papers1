Home/Build a portfolio

Lost your certificates? Build proof of your skills without them.

Answer questions about the work you have actually done. You get a printable portfolio with your skills, case studies and the people who can confirm your work, plus a plan for recovering your documents. What you type is not sent anywhere.

What you will need

  • Your work history: where you worked and for how long
  • The name and phone number of a supervisor and a colleague
  • Photos of your work or documents, if you have any

What you will get

  • A portfolio built only from what you wrote
  • A declaration and space for your referees to sign
  • Steps for recovering your missing documents

Build your portfolio

    Your details

    A clear photo helps people recognise you. It does not replace an ID.

    Your skills

    What have you done in practice?

    Be accurate. Only claim what a former colleague could confirm.

    Practical verification

    Answer real scenarios from your profession in your own words. They show how you think and work, and an assessor can follow them up in an interview or test. Skip any you prefer not to answer.

    Your work

    Case studies

    Describe up to six pieces of real work: the problem, what you did and the result. Add photos of the work if you have them.

    People and documents

    People who can confirm your work

    Open a person to add their details. Skip anyone you do not have. Ask their permission before you share their details.

    Your documents

    Open a document to add its details and attach a photo or copy. Tick the box if you no longer have the original. Skip documents you do not need to mention.

    What happened to your documents?

    In your own words, say how you lost them. This is optional. It appears in your portfolio so that an authority understands your situation.

    Review and build

    Check your answers and confirm that they are true. You can go back and change anything before you build your portfolio.

    Privacy policy

    Effective November 24, 2026.

    What this site does with your information

    Everything you type into the portfolio form, and any photos or files you attach, is processed in your browser. Attached files are read on your device only. They are not uploaded or saved, and they disappear when you close or refresh the page, so print or save your portfolio as a PDF before you leave. The site does not send it to a server, store it in a database, or share it with anyone. When you close or refresh the page, it is gone.

    What we do not use

    This site does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. It does not ask you to create an account.

    What the hosting provider may record

    Like any website, the company that hosts it may keep standard technical logs, such as your IP address and the time of your visit. This is outside the control of the site and is covered by the host’s own policy.

    Printing and copying

    If you print the portfolio, save it as a PDF or copy the letter, that copy is stored wherever you put it. Keep it somewhere safe, because it may include your name, workplace and country, and the names and phone numbers of the people you list as referees. Ask those people for permission before you share their details.

    Children

    This site is intended for adults seeking recognition of their professional skills.

    Terms and conditions

    Effective November 24, 2026.

    What this tool is

    Beyond Paper helps you organize a description of your professional experience and a list of ways to recover evidence of it. It is a self-assessment tool. It does not verify your qualifications.

    Not legal or official advice

    The portfolio and plan are general guidance. They are not legal advice, and they are not an official credential assessment. Rules for recognizing qualifications differ by country, profession and authority. Always confirm requirements with the authority that will assess you before paying any fee.

    No guarantee

    Using this tool does not guarantee that any authority, employer or institution will recognize your skills or qualifications.

    Your responsibility

    You are responsible for the accuracy of what you enter. Only present experience you have genuinely had. Submitting false claims to an authority or employer can have serious consequences.

    The site is provided as it is. It may change or be unavailable at times.
